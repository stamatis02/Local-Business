How a project runs

Four clear stages, from the first call to launch day. You always know what happens next.

01020304 /04
  1. Concept, strategy & design
  2. Development & coding
  3. Testing & quality assurance
  4. Going live & support
Step 01

Concept, strategy & design

We start with a call about your business, your customers and what the site needs to achieve. I map the pages, plan the content and design every screen before a line of code is written. You approve the design first.

  • Discovery call
  • Sitemap & wireframes
  • Full visual design

Typically week 1

Step 02

Development & coding

I build the approved design into a fast, hand-coded site: responsive on every screen, animated where it helps, and simple for you to update. You get a private preview link to follow along.

  • Clean, hand-written code
  • Responsive on every device
  • SEO foundations

Typically weeks 2–3

Step 03

Testing & quality assurance

Every page is checked on real phones, tablets and browsers. I test speed, forms, accessibility and search basics, and fix what I find, so nothing breaks on launch day.

  • Cross-device testing
  • Speed & Lighthouse audit
  • Accessibility checks

Typically a few days

Step 04

Going live & support

I connect your domain, launch the site and make sure Google can find it. After launch I stay on hand for questions, updates and fixes, so you're never left alone with it.

  • Domain & hosting setup
  • Analytics
  • 30 days of free support

Launch day, then ongoing
